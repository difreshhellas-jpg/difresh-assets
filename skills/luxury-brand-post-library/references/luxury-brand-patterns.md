## 1. Nike - Bold Minimalism

**Brand DNA:** Empowering, direct, actionable, athlete-focused

### Visual Architecture
- **Layout:** Asymmetric hero (product/athlete left or center) + bold typography right
- **Composition:** High contrast, spacious, uncluttered
- **Spacing:** Massive negative space; breathing room between elements
- **Image Style:** Athlete/product in motion or striking pose; dramatic lighting; clean backgrounds

### Color Palette
- Primary: Black (`#000000`), White (`#FFFFFF`)
- Accent: Vibrant color (electric blue `#0055FF`, fire red `#FF0000`, neon green `#00FF00`)
- Mood: High contrast, energetic, bold

### Typography
- Headline: Heavy sans-serif (Helvetica Neue Bold, Futura Bold)
- Font size: LARGE (80-120px for headlines)
- Weight: Bold (700+)
- Case: All caps common ("JUST DO IT")
- Body: Clean sans-serif, much smaller (20-30px)

### Copy Tone
- **Register:** Direct, motivational, action-oriented
- **Sentence structure:** Short. Punchy. Impactful.
- **Emotional appeal:** Empowerment, achievement, "just do it"
- **Examples:**
  - "Don't dream. Do."
  - "The only impossible is the one you don't attempt."
  - "Built for the ones who push."

### Copy Structure
```
HEADLINE (motivational, one line, imperative)
[Hero image takes up 60-70% of space]
Brief subtext (who/what this is for)
Link in bio
```

### Post Strategy
- **Frequency:** 2-4x per week
- **Best time:** Morning (7-9am, motivation time) or evening pre-workout (5-7pm)
- **Post type:** Single image + Reel combos
- **Engagement driver:** Aspirational, actionable, speaks to achievement mindset

### Hashtag Strategy
- Nike uses branded hashtags (#JustDoIt, #NikeLab) + sport/category tags
- Example: #JustDoIt #NikeRunning #RunnersMindset

### Examples (Concepts)
- Single shot of new shoe, massive "LAUNCH" header
- Athlete mid-action, headline "READY" with minimal text below
- Clean product shot on white background with typography overlay

## 2. Chanel - Timeless Elegance

**Brand DNA:** Heritage, craftsmanship, luxury, understated power, French elegance

### Visual Architecture
- **Layout:** Centered, symmetrical, classical
- **Composition:** Often close-up product or lifestyle intimate moment
- **Spacing:** Intentional, refined, never cluttered
- **Image Style:** High-fashion photography; soft lighting; luxe textures; often monochrome or tonal

### Color Palette
- Primary: Black (`#000000`), Cream (`#F5F5F5`), Off-white (`#EFEFEF`)
- Accent: Gold (`#D4AF37`), Nude (`#E8D5C4`)
- Secondary: Pearl gray (`#E8E8E8`), Taupe (`#B39B7E`)
- Mood: Sophisticated, timeless, understated

### Typography
- Headline: Classic serif (Garamond, Didot, Bodoni)
- Font size: Medium-large (60-90px)
- Weight: Regular to medium (400-600)
- Case: Title case, elegant
- Body: Serif or refined sans-serif, 16-24px

### Copy Tone
- **Register:** Sophisticated, understated, narrative
- **Sentence structure:** Flowing, poetic, longer (storytelling)
- **Emotional appeal:** Heritage, craftsmanship, timelessness, French elegance
- **Examples:**
  - "Timeless elegance. Perpetual allure."
  - "The N°5 code. Since 1921."
  - "Crafted for those who know."

### Copy Structure
```
ELEGANT HEADLINE (serif, understated)
[Hero image - close-up, intimate, luxe]
Heritage/craft narrative (2-3 sentences)
"Learn more" or soft CTA
```

### Post Strategy
- **Frequency:** 1-2x per week (quality over quantity)
- **Best time:** Afternoon (2-4pm, luxury browsing time)
- **Post type:** Single image, carousel (multiple details)
- **Engagement driver:** Heritage storytelling, heritage codes (N°5, Quilted Bag), exclusivity

### Hashtag Strategy
- Branded only: #Chanel, #ChanelOfficial
- Rarely uses trending hashtags (too common)
- Example: #Chanel #Timeless #LuxuryCouture

### Examples (Concepts)
- Close-up of quilted bag detail, headline "Quilted. Since 1955."
- Monochrome image of classic suit jacket, copy about craftsmanship
- Timeline visual: "86 years of N°5"

## 3. Dior - Dramatic Maximalism

**Brand DNA:** Haute couture, artistic, seasonal, dramatic, fashion-forward

### Visual Architecture
- **Layout:** Full-bleed hero (image takes entire space) OR split dynamic
- **Composition:** Theatrical, cinematic, movement
- **Spacing:** Bold, no fear of color or texture
- **Image Style:** High-fashion photography; dramatic lighting; saturated colors; model/product prominent

### Color Palette
- Primary: Deep red (`#8B0000`), Black (`#000000`), Gold (`#FFD700`)
- Seasonal: Bold variations (spring pastels, summer brights, fall burgundies)
- Secondary: Cream (`#FFF8DC`), Charcoal (`#36454F`)
- Mood: Dramatic, artistic, seasonal, bold

### Typography
- Headline: Bold serif (Bodoni, Didot) or dramatic sans-serif
- Font size: LARGE (70-120px)
- Weight: Bold (700+) or thin elegant (300) for contrast
- Case: Mixed (title case, all caps for drama)
- Body: Refined serif or sans-serif, 18-28px

### Copy Tone
- **Register:** Artistic, narrative, poetic, seasonal
- **Sentence structure:** Flowing, evocative, emotional
- **Emotional appeal:** Fashion artistry, seasonal celebration, exclusivity, haute couture
- **Examples:**
  - "The beauty of excess. The art of couture."
  - "Dare to be bold. Dare to be Dior."
  - "A new silhouette. A new story."

### Copy Structure
```
DRAMATIC HEADLINE (serif, bold)
[Full-bleed or hero image, cinematic]
Narrative copy (2-4 sentences, poetic)
"Discover" or "Explore" soft CTA
```

### Post Strategy
- **Frequency:** 2-3x per week (fashion cycle)
- **Best time:** Evening (6-8pm, aspirational browsing)
- **Post type:** Single hero image, Carousel (tells fashion story)
- **Engagement driver:** Seasonal changes, haute couture exclusivity, artistic direction

### Hashtag Strategy
- #Dior, #DiorCouture, #DiorOfficial
- Seasonal hashtags (#DiorSpring2024, #DiorSummer)
- Example: #Dior #DiorCouture #FashionArtistry

### Examples (Concepts)
- Full-bleed runway model shot, headline "NEW SILHOUETTE. NEW POWER."
- Seasonal color palette reveal (red/gold for fall), artistic direction statement
- Detail shot of couture embroidery, copy on craftsmanship

## 4. Valentino - Romantic Editorialization

**Brand DNA:** Romantic, editorial, artistic, couture, deep creative vision

### Visual Architecture
- **Layout:** Cinematic, asymmetric, mixed scale elements
- **Composition:** Theatrical, editorial magazine style, mixed proportions
- **Spacing:** Intentional, artistic, playful
- **Image Style:** Artistic photography; mixed subjects; editorial layout feel; romantic lighting

### Color Palette
- Primary: Deep red (`#8B0000`), Black (`#000000`), Gold (`#D4AF37`)
- Secondary: Blush (`#F08080`), Cream (`#FFF5EE`)
- Mood: Romantic, deep, artistic, editorial

### Typography
- Headline: Mixed scale serif (large + small) or elegant sans-serif
- Font size: Varied (60-100px headline, but mix with small accents 12-18px)
- Weight: Mix regular and bold for contrast
- Case: Title case, artistic
- Body: Serif or decorative, 16-26px

### Copy Tone
- **Register:** Artistic, emotional, narrative, romantic
- **Sentence structure:** Flowing, poetic, expressive
- **Emotional appeal:** Artistic vision, romance, emotional depth, creative journey
- **Examples:**
  - "In red, we trust."
  - "Emotion wrapped in elegance."
  - "A love story, tailored."

### Copy Structure
```
ARTISTIC HEADLINE (mixed size, serif)
[Editorial-style image, artistic composition]
Emotional narrative (2-3 sentences)
Soft exploration CTA ("Read the story", "See more")
```

### Post Strategy
- **Frequency:** 2-3x per week (tied to collections)
- **Best time:** Evening (7-9pm, deep engagement time)
- **Post type:** Carousel (editorial journey), single artistic image
- **Engagement driver:** Artistic story, emotional connection, fashion artistry

### Hashtag Strategy
- #Valentino, #ValentinoBeauty
- Artistic/emotional tags (#RomanticFashion, #EditorialStyle)
- Example: #Valentino #RomanticFashion #ArtisticVision

### Examples (Concepts)
- Runway photo with hand-lettered "RED" overlaid in large serif type
- Mixed-scale carousel: hero + detail shots + artist quote
- Behind-the-scenes editorial: designer + atelier + finished piece

## 5. Givenchy - Minimal Luxury

**Brand DNA:** Refined, architectural, minimalist, technical beauty, understated power

### Visual Architecture
- **Layout:** Clean grid, geometric, centered or one-third rule
- **Composition:** Minimalist, lots of negative space, architectural
- **Spacing:** Generous, breathing room, intentional every pixel
- **Image Style:** Clean product photography; geometric lighting; often monochrome or tonal

### Color Palette
- Primary: Black (`#000000`), White (`#FFFFFF`), Charcoal gray (`#36454F`)
- Secondary: Taupe (`#B39B7E`), Pearl (`#E8E8E8`)
- Mood: Refined, minimal, architectural, technical

### Typography
- Headline: Minimal sans-serif (Helvetica, Univers, custom clean fonts)
- Font size: Medium (50-80px)
- Weight: Regular (400) or bold (700) for contrast
- Case: Title case or lowercase (intentional)
- Body: Clean sans-serif, 14-20px

### Copy Tone
- **Register:** Refined, technical, poetic minimalism
- **Sentence structure:** Short, precise, sometimes fragmented
- **Emotional appeal:** Technical beauty, architectural thinking, refined taste, "less is more"
- **Examples:**
  - "Form meets function."
  - "Minimal. Intentional. Timeless."
  - "Beauty in simplicity."

### Copy Structure
```
MINIMAL HEADLINE (sans-serif, simple)
[Clean product image, architectural framing]
Brief, precise copy (1-2 sentences max)
Soft or no CTA (let design speak)
```

### Post Strategy
- **Frequency:** 1-2x per week (quality over frequency)
- **Best time:** Afternoon (3-5pm, sophisticated browsing)
- **Post type:** Single image (let minimalism speak)
- **Engagement driver:** Design sophistication, technical craftsmanship, architectural thinking

### Hashtag Strategy
- #Givenchy, #GivenchyOfficial
- Minimal/design tags (#MinimalLuxury, #ArchitecturalDesign)
- Example: #Givenchy #MinimalLuxury #DesignThinking

### Examples (Concepts)
- Single clothing item, three-quarters white space, small elegant headline
- Architectural detail shot (zipper, seam), copy "Precision. Every. Detail."
- Grid of product shots in monochrome, minimal layout

## 6. Hermès - Artisanal Storytelling

**Brand DNA:** Craftsmanship, heritage, artisanal, storytelling, timeless, French heritage

### Visual Architecture
- **Layout:** Narrative, lifestyle, intimate
- **Composition:** Often documentary/editorial feel, shows hands/craft, intimate
- **Spacing:** Balanced, warm, inviting
- **Image Style:** Lifestyle photography; artisan at work; material close-ups; warm lighting

### Color Palette
- Primary: Hermès orange (`#FF6D00`), Warm brown (`#8B6F47`), Cream (`#FFF5E6`)
- Secondary: Taupe (`#A39B8B`), Warm gold (`#C9A24B`)
- Mood: Warm, artisanal, heritage, inviting

### Typography
- Headline: Serif or elegant sans-serif
- Font size: Medium (55-85px)
- Weight: Regular (400) to medium (600)
- Case: Title case, elegant
- Body: Serif, warm, 16-24px

### Copy Tone
- **Register:** Narrative, heritage-focused, artisanal, storytelling
- **Sentence structure:** Flowing, documentary style, educational
- **Emotional appeal:** Craftsmanship, heritage, artisanal values, "made by hand", timelessness
- **Examples:**
  - "Made by our artisans since 1837."
  - "In the hands of a craftsperson, leather becomes art."
  - "Where tradition meets precision."

### Copy Structure
```
HERITAGE HEADLINE (serif, warm)
[Image of craft/artisan/hands/material]
Storytelling narrative (2-3 sentences)
"Discover the craft" soft CTA
```

### Post Strategy
- **Frequency:** 1-2x per week (artisanal, not rushed)
- **Best time:** Morning/midday (9am-12pm, lifestyle time)
- **Post type:** Single image + carousel (process story)
- **Engagement driver:** Artisanal story, craftsmanship details, heritage codes, "made by hands"

### Hashtag Strategy
- #Hermes, #HermesOfficial
- Artisanal tags (#Craftmanship, #MadeByHand, #Heritage)
- Example: #Hermes #Craftsmanship #HeritageCraft

### Examples (Concepts)
- Artisan hands stitching leather, warm lighting, headline "Made by hand"
- Material detail (leather grain, orange box), story of sourcing
- Multi-shot carousel: raw materials → craft process → finished piece

## 7. Louis Vuitton - Monogram Power

**Brand DNA:** Iconic monogram, travel/lifestyle, luxury aspirational, globally recognized, timeless

### Visual Architecture
- **Layout:** Mixed (monogram feature + lifestyle scene OR product-centric)
- **Composition:** Bold, confident, globally aspirational
- **Spacing:** Balanced, lets monogram breathe or dominate
- **Image Style:** High-fashion; travel/lifestyle; monogram prominent; often warm/gold-toned

### Color Palette
- Primary: Monogram brown (`#8B6F47`), Gold (`#D4AF37`), Cream (`#F5F5DC`)
- Secondary: Black (`#000000`), Warm tan (`#A39B8B`)
- Mood: Aspirational, warm luxury, globally iconic

### Typography
- Headline: Serif or LV-custom (bold, strong)
- Font size: Large (65-95px)
- Weight: Bold (700) for impact
- Case: Title case or all caps
- Body: Serif, 18-26px

### Copy Tone
- **Register:** Aspirational, global, iconic, lifestyle
- **Sentence structure:** Flowing, lifestyle narrative
- **Emotional appeal:** Iconic status, travel aspirations, timeless luxury, "made for the world"
- **Examples:**
  - "Travel in iconic elegance."
  - "Recognized. Beloved. Timeless."
  - "The world carries LV."

### Copy Structure
```
ICONIC HEADLINE (serif, bold)
[Image featuring monogram OR lifestyle with product]
Aspirational lifestyle copy (1-2 sentences)
"Shop" or "Discover" CTA
```

### Post Strategy
- **Frequency:** 2-3x per week (accessible luxury)
- **Best time:** Morning/afternoon (10am-2pm, shopping consideration)
- **Post type:** Single image, carousel (lifestyle journey)
- **Engagement driver:** Monogram recognition, travel aspirations, iconic status

### Hashtag Strategy
- #LouisVuitton, #LV, #LouisVuittonOfficial
- Travel/lifestyle tags (#TravelInStyle, #IconicStyle)
- Example: #LouisVuitton #TravelInStyle #IconicLuxury

### Examples (Concepts)
- Monogram canvas with headline "RECOGNIZED WORLDWIDE"
- Traveler with LV bag in exotic location
- Campaign: close-up monogram + aspirational lifestyle moment

## 8. Application Strategy for DIFRESH

### Matching Your Brief to a Brand

**Use Nike for:**
- Bold announcements
- Performance/achievement focused
- Direct calls to action
- Motivational messaging

**Use Chanel for:**
- Heritage storytelling
- Craft/process posts
- Timeless positioning
- French elegance

**Use Dior for:**
- Seasonal campaigns
- Bold creative work
- New launches
- Artistic direction statements

**Use Valentino for:**
- Creative projects
- Emotional storytelling
- Behind-the-scenes narratives
- Artistic collaborations

**Use Givenchy for:**
- Design thinking posts
- Technical/craft details
- Minimalist announcements
- Architectural or structured work

**Use Hermès for:**
- Artisanal process posts
- Craft storytelling
- Heritage/tradition
- "Made by hands" narratives

**Use Louis Vuitton for:**
- Global reach announcements
- Aspirational positioning
- Travel/expansion announcements
- Iconic brand moments

### DIFRESH Blended Approach

For DIFRESH specifically, blend:
- **Givenchy minimalism** (clean, architectural, intentional) for core brand posts
- **Chanel heritage** (craft, storytelling) for process/behind-the-scenes
- **Valentino artistry** (creative, editorial) for creative projects
- **Hermès craftsmanship** (hands-on, narrative) for automation/tool announcements

**Color palette to use across all:**
- Dark base (black/charcoal from Givenchy)
- Gold accent (Chanel/Dior luxury)
- Warm tones (Hermès artisanal feel)
- White negative space (Nike/Givenchy minimalism)
