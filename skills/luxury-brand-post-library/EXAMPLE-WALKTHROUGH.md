# Luxury Brand Post Library - Complete Example Walkthrough

## Real-World Scenario: Bistrot Creperie Menu Redesign

This is a complete example showing how to use the skill from start to finish for an actual DIFRESH project.

---

## 📌 The Situation

**What:** Bistrot Creperie menu was redesigned from 5 pages → 4 pages  
**Design:** Dark chalkboard aesthetic, baroque gold frames, antique gold accents (#C9A24B)  
**Goal:** Showcase the design quality AND tell the story of the creative condensing process  
**Audience:** Instagram followers (design-minded, appreciate craftsmanship)  

---

## 🚀 Step 1: Create Your Brief

Write a **specific, detailed brief** that captures:
- What you're posting (what is it?)
- Visual/brand direction (style, aesthetic, mood)
- Story/message (what are you trying to say?)
- Goal (awareness? engagement? positioning? traffic?)

```
DIFRESH Brief:
"Bistrot Creperie Menu Redesign Showcase

What: We redesigned Bistrot Creperie's menu from 5 pages down to 4 
pages. It's a dark chalkboard aesthetic with baroque frame corners 
and antique gold accents (#C9A24B). The design is intentional and 
refined - every detail matters.

Style: Dark, elegant, French bistro luxury, handcrafted feeling

Story: Show both the final design AND the creative challenge/process 
of condensing 5 pages into 4 while maintaining elegance and usability.

Goal: Position DIFRESH as premium design studio that thinks deeply 
about constraints and elegance. Get saves and shares from design community."
```

## 🎨 Step 4: Design in Canva

**Using the spec, create a Canva design:**

### Layout
- Split asymmetric: 60% image (left), 40% copy (right)
- OR use carousel (5 slides following the flow above)

### Colors
- Primary background: `#FFF5E6` (cream)
- Text: `#0A0908` (charcoal)
- Accents: `#C9A24B` (gold)
- Warm neutrals: `#8B8680`

### Typography
- Headline: Cormorant Display, 48px, bold, charcoal
- Subheading: Playfair Display, 24px, taupe
- Body: DM Sans, 14px, taupe, 1.6 line height

### Images
1. Hero: Final menu design (closed, framed, gold detail prominent)
2. Detail 1: Interior spread showing layout
3. Detail 2: Baroque frame corner close-up
4. Process: Original sketch/5-page version

### Design Details
- Lots of white space (sophisticated)
- Gold accents used sparingly (luxury, not overdone)
- Frame the menu photo like it's gallery art
- Typography hierarchy clear (headline reads first, then supporting)

## 📸 Step 6: Source/Shoot Images

**Image Checklist (from spec):**

✅ **Hero Image:** Final menu, closed, framed beautifully  
- Studio lighting (key light from upper left)
- High contrast (charcoal background)
- Frame detail visible (baroque curve + gold)
- Professional, gallery-quality

✅ **Detail 1:** Menu interior open  
- Shows 4-page layout
- Readable content
- Consistent lighting

✅ **Detail 2:** Frame corner close-up  
- Baroque curves prominent
- Gold catching light
- Shows craftsmanship

✅ **Detail 3:** Original or sketch  
- Shows "before" (5 pages)
- Creates before/after narrative
- Sketches or rough layouts work

**Mood:** Professional, elegant, museum-quality, warm-lit, intentional
