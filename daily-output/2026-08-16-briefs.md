**Universal compositor rules for every asset in this batch**

- Position all type by **ink bounding box**, never by font metrics. Measure the rendered glyph
  extents and place from those.
- Minimum margin from any frame edge to any ink: **64px** (story: the safe zones in ST below).
- Headline ink width ceiling **620px** on the 1080 canvas. Headline cap height ceiling **200px**.
  Never let a headline bleed past an edge and never clip it. If a string overruns, reduce the size
  to the measure, do not overflow and do not crop.
- Greek strings carry descenders on beta, gamma, rho, chi, psi and on accented lowercase. Reserve
  descender room in the ink box; do not set Greek on a tight metric line.
- **Assert no intersection** between the plate rectangle and the type zone before drawing. The two
  rectangles are given numerically per slot. If they overlap, the layout is wrong, stop.
- **Contrast check against real pixels.** Sample the actual rendered pixels under every text ink box
  after the plate is placed and verify the stated text colour clears 4.5:1 against them. Do not
  assume the ground colour is what sits behind the type.
- **Ground bleed.** After placing the plate, bleed the ground colour into the outer edge of the
  image by 4 to 8 percent so plate and field read as one manufactured surface. Feather it, do not
  draw a border.
- One ground, one text value, one accent per frame. No fourth value, no opacity variants of the
  text colour.
- The wordmark DIFRESH appears once on every asset, at the position, size and typeface specified
  for that slot only. Do not lock it to a shared corner.
- No eyebrows anywhere in this batch (zero across all seven). No hairline grids, crosshairs,
  registration marks, section numbers or bulletin stamps. No emoji. No decorative dots.
- Posts render at **1080x1350**. Story renders at **1080x1920**.

**Universal image generation rules**

- Generate at the stated target aspect ratio directly. Never generate square or arbitrary and crop
  to fit. If the generator returns the wrong shape, regenerate with an explicit aspect ratio
  instruction.
- The named reference photograph is a **mandatory input**. The product in the generated frame must
  be the same product with the same packaging and the same distinguishing features. Do not let the
  generator invent a different bottle shape, a different cap, a different colour count or a
  different graphic.
- **No added text, no captions, no watermarks, no logos, no wordmarks, no numbers and no signage in
  the generated image.** The only lettering permitted anywhere in the frame is the product's own
  printed label exactly as it appears on the real product in the reference. Type is composited
  afterwards.
- No cream, bone, chalk, beige, brass, ochre or espresso grading in any generated frame. Where a
  reference scene is warm, regrade it cold or saturated per the slot's stated direction.
