## RULES THAT BIND THE LOCAL COMPOSITOR

These apply to every entry below and are not repeated per slot.

1. **Aspect ratio.** Posts generate at 1080x1350. The story generates at 1080x1920. If a generation
   returns any other shape, regenerate with an explicit aspect ratio instruction. Never crop.
2. **Plates are placed whole.** The generated frame is scaled proportionally into the plate
   rectangle given below and is never trimmed, bled off an edge, keyed out, or feathered. Every
   plate rectangle below is already at the generated image's own aspect ratio, so a proportional
   scale lands exactly.
3. **Ink bounding box positioning.** All y values below are ink box positions, not font metric
   positions. Anchor a wrapped block by the FIRST line's ink box top, then advance on a constant
   baseline grid, so a Greek line with no ascenders does not get pushed down.
4. **No intersection.** Compute the plate rectangle and every type block rectangle, then assert
   they do not intersect. Type never sits on the generated image. Also assert no two type blocks
   intersect each other, headline against caption included.
5. **Contrast check against rendered pixels.** Sample the actual composited pixels behind every
   text run and verify the stated ratio. Do not assume the ground colour is what is behind the
   glyphs.
6. **Grading.** Bleed the frame's ground colour into the outer 40px of the plate on all four edges
   at 4 to 8 percent opacity, so plate and field read as one manufactured surface. Measure it, do
   not eyeball it.
7. **Margins.** No type element closer than 64px to any canvas edge. Headline ink width must not
   exceed 620px on the 1080 canvas and headline cap height must not exceed 200px.
8. **Greek verification.** Slots 01 and 04 are Greek. Verify the named heading and body fonts have
   full Greek cmap coverage before rendering, then verify the rendered PNG by eye for tofu boxes or
   fallback substitution. Jura, Tektur, Roboto Mono and Fira Sans all carry Greek; confirm anyway.
9. **Colour discipline.** One ground, one text value, one accent per frame. No fourth colour. Every
   accent below is specified as a graphic element, never as text, unless stated otherwise.
10. **No baked in type.** Every image prompt below ends with an explicit instruction that the
    generated image must contain no text, no lettering, no captions, no watermarks, no logos and no
    wordmarks. The product's own moulded or printed packaging is part of the product and stays.
    Nothing typographic is added to it.
